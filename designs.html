Self-Driving Lab (SDL) System Design

Comprehensive system design from electrical architecture to hardware layout. I designed, assembled, and programmed the entire self-driving lab system—including all hardware, electronics, and software (except the active learning algorithm). The SDL integrates Python, Arduino, sensor networks, and multi-microcontroller control for automated material composition, curing analysis, and tensile testing. The system is fully operational and actively used for research at Georgia Tech.

See SDL project page for more details and demonstrations.

Closed loop PID Liquid Dispensing System

Automated liquid dispensing platform featuring closed-loop PID control for precise volume delivery. The purpose is to dispense specific amounts of liquid monomer and crosslinker for the self-driving lab.This system uses an Arduino, peristaltic pumps, stepper motor drivers, and real-time sensor feedback to achieve high accuracy and repeatability.

Powder Dispenser

The powder dispenser is designed to dispense photoinitiators into photopolymer inks during the processing portion of the self-driving lab. The system uses a stepper motor to turn gears that allow powder to be dispensed. The speed of the motor and the number of revolutions determine the amount of powder dispensed. Curing Time System Testing Platform

The curing time system platform is designed to test the curing properties of photopolymer inks as a part of the self-driving lab. Springs compress the two hands to secure a container of photopolymer ink. There are also integrated UV LEDs which are used to cure the ink.
