Powder Dispenser Design Process

A detailed look at the iterative design process for the automated powder dispensing system.

1

Initial Concept Design

Initial sketch exploring the stepper motor powder dispensing mechanism. This concept established the basic geometry and component layout for automated powder dispensing in the self-driving lab.

2

First CAD Iteration

Initial CAD design to bring the sketch to life. The stepper motor was chosen because of the control capabilities. Some limitations were found with the gears 3D printability and the funnel being too rough.

3

Prototype Testing

Dispenser testing with updated gears and the addition of a vibrating motor to allow the powder to flow out of the funnel.

✓

Final Implementation and Modeling

The completed powder dispenser successfully integrated into the self-driving lab's material composition system. The relationship between the stepper motor gear speed and the amount of powder dispensed was tested and modeled to allow interpolation of any powder amount.

Key Learnings

  • Mechanism Design: Stepper motor driven systems required careful adjustments for consistent performance
  • Material Compatibility: Different powder types would have different flow rates and would require different models
  • Automation Integration: Final design successfully integrated with lab automation systems to allow for the powder to be dispensed in a controlled manner.
